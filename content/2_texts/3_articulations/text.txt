Title: Articulations

----

Author: Allison Parrish

----

Text: <p><u>She went away, away, she went away. She went away.</u></p><p>Thunder, lightning, fire and rain, and laughter, and inn-fires. After the fire of London and another of a pastoral vein: — the venerable original the adolescent and the venerable, richly the upland and the vale adorn, buddha, the holy and benevolent, of many a lover, who the heaven would think who have made me thus unworthy of a name or maybe there, like many another one and many a worthy dame.</p><p>And many a wonder seen: many and many a winter day and many a winter day on many a windmill turning in many a winding loose meander, in many a wood and many a field, with many a pull and many a fee, with many and many a call; with many a halt and many a call, with many a sigh and many a tear, many a sigh, and many a tear, many and many a year.</p><p>For many and many a year. Of many a land for many a year, for many a year, and many an Age, for many a year, on many a distant main — Of many a race, and many a name, in many a song, and many a merry Tale many a man and many a maid, many a man and maiden!</p><p>It stirred of many a man and many a maid, honoured of many among men.</p><p>The name of many a sturdy one while many a minister below, when I believed in many a mystery by many a green familiar lane full many a mighty name many a merry meeting we ran him at many a meeting on many women many men rely many, many welcomes, young man, anyway.</p><p>Any one else. Nor any one else. Nor shall any one where she knelt. In friendly way, when she wanes and she went away.</p><p>And then she went away. She went away, away, she went away. She went away — went whistling too, she went away with song, away with silks, away with lawn, away with fear, away!</p><p>Away, away with the witch of spray away — away — without a wing, Wasting away — away — away, away, away, away!</p><p>Away, away, and away, away, and away, and away. Away and away. And away — away, a-way, a-way, then — away. Away. Then away — away — away. Away, away. Bear me away, away, and bear me away! — away!</p><p>Away, away, and anywhere. We were weary, and we still away, away we went, — far away, while low we weep.</p><p>O Willie was a witty wight... Why was Charlotte there!</p><p>But the sea was far away. Was far away. Is far away, And far away far and far away: that far and far away.</p><p>And far away and far away — far away, far away. Far, far away; far away. Far, far away; far, far away; far, far away.</p><p>From very far away... every road and every street and every bird and every deer, and every river in her bed from every danger, every foe, forever and ever, amen!</p><p><strong>Forever and amen. And ever. Amen.</strong></p><p>Must ever bleed! And ever be blest and ever be the best.</p><p>Stronger than steel smothered in straw. Smothered in snow. Under the snow; under the stone...</p><p>So mournful, yet so sweet. So sweet. So magical! — so slight, so sweet, so sad...</p><p>So kind, so true — oh, so true! Ah, so kind.</p><p>What’s your name. What is your will? Or what you will...</p><p>Say will you marry me, my pretty maid...</p><p>Many, many years — how many, many years ago.</p><p>O lost, beloved. I loved it so long...</p><p>To hear the story told he told a story to her...</p><p>Then lo, and behold. Mellow and slow...</p><p>Into a field of wheat. In the fields of May!</p><p>In the fields of Heaven... of uneventful years; and even love of wine, of evidence well enough.</p>

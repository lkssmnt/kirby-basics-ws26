Text: <p>This bachelor’s thesis is dedicated to the question of how we exist in cyberspace and what this existence can look like. A video and an accompanying catalog present an interpretation of a ‘virtual body,’ drawing attention to the lack of awareness of our movement in the virtual world and the digital traces we leave behind.</p>
